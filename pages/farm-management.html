About PoultryGIS

PoultryGIS is a GIS-based decision-support application for poultry production planning in Mashonaland East, Zimbabwe.

The application combines spatial proximity analysis with information on poultry markets, input suppliers, veterinary services, hatcheries and water resources.

Spatial Analysis

  • Haversine distance calculation
  • Nearest-feature analysis
  • Market accessibility
  • Estimated travel time
  • Market accessibility within 60 minutes
  • Water proximity
  • Veterinary-service proximity
  • Supplier proximity
  • Hatchery proximity
  • Site suitability scoring
